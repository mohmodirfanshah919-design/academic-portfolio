2026Journal paper

Comparative analysis of different-shaped interfering footings placed on geocell-reinforced silty sand

Zuha Kanji, Shibayan Biswas, Swaraj Chowdhury

Geomechanics and Engineering, 44(1), 79–104. January 2026.

Abstract

This study uses three-dimensional numerical analysis to compare circular and square interfering footings of equal area on geocell-reinforced silty sand. It examines the influence of footing spacing and geocell configuration on ultimate bearing capacity. In the investigated cases, the circular footing showed higher bearing performance, while reinforcement produced improvements that varied with spacing and geocell geometry.

2024Book chapter

Numerical analysis of circular footing on geocell-reinforced pond ash bed

Zuha Kanji, Shibayan Biswas, Swaraj Chowdhury

Proceedings of the Indian Geotechnical Conference (IGC 2024), Volume 6, pp. 75–84. Springer.

Abstract

This chapter investigates the load–settlement behaviour of a circular footing on unreinforced and geocell-reinforced pond ash using PLAXIS 3D finite-element analysis. The study considers reinforcement geometry and placement, including depth, width and height, to assess the effect on bearing capacity. The authors report improvements in bearing capacity across the modelled reinforcement configurations.
