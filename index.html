CONFERENCE PROCEEDINGS · BOOK CHAPTER

Numerical Analysis of Circular Footing on Geocell-Reinforced Pond Ash Bed

Zuha Kanji, Shibayan Biswas, and Swaraj Chowdhury · Proceedings of the Indian Geotechnical Conference (IGC 2024), Volume 6 · pp. 75–84

Read abstract ＋

This study examines the load–settlement behaviour of a circular footing on unreinforced and geocell-reinforced pond ash using finite-element analysis in PLAXIS 3D. The geocell geometry was modelled using AutoCAD, and the analysis considered reinforcement depth, width, and height. The results identify effective reinforcement configurations and indicate that geocell reinforcement can improve the ultimate bearing capacity by approximately 36.14%–134.42% compared with an unreinforced pond ash bed.

JOURNAL ARTICLE · GEOMECHANICS AND ENGINEERING

Comparative Analysis of Different-Shaped Interfering Footings Placed on Geocell-Reinforced Silty Sand

Zuha Kanji, Shibayan Biswas, and Swaraj Chowdhury · Geomechanics and Engineering, 44(1), pp. 79–104

Read abstract ＋

This study uses three-dimensional finite-element simulations to compare circular and square interfering footings of equivalent area on geocell-reinforced silty sand. It evaluates footing spacing, geocell height and aperture size, and the strength and stiffness of the soil. The results indicate that geocell reinforcement improves ultimate bearing capacity, with circular footings showing higher values than square footings in the studied cases. The reported improvement for circular footings ranges from 12.85% to 14.68% with changes in spacing ratio, and from 2.35% to 12.98% across geocell configurations.
